Care Built Around
You, Not Your Insurance

Dr. Zach Beims is a 2019 graduate of Cleveland Chiropractic College with years of hands-on experience treating patients of all ages. He moved to Hesston in January 2025 with his wife and three kids, and opened Hesston Family Chiropractic a year later — because he believes every small town deserves access to great, affordable care without the insurance headache.
